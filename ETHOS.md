# Ethos

## Why this exists

Meetings eat time. Most of them don't need you — they need your knowledge, your answers, your voice. This project gives you that back.

AI Meeting Avatar is a tool that lets an AI attend calls on your behalf. It listens, it thinks, it speaks. You stay focused on actual work.

## Principles

### Your machine, your data

The default setup runs entirely on your Mac. Speech recognition, AI reasoning, voice synthesis — all local. No audio leaves your device. No cloud account required. No telemetry. No tracking.

If you choose the Claude brain, that's an explicit opt-in. The local Gemma brain exists so you never have to.

### Simple over clever

Every feature should work with one command. The setup wizard asks two questions and handles the rest. If someone needs to read a wall of docs to get started, we failed.

### Honest about what it is

This is not a deepfake tool. It's not pretending to be you in a way that deceives. It's an AI assistant that speaks in a meeting room — and everyone in that room knows it's an AI. The voice is synthetic. The responses identify as an AI assistant.

We build tools that augment people, not tools that impersonate them.

### Local first, cloud optional

Cloud features (Claude brain, LiveKit Cloud, future integrations) are always optional add-ons. The core experience works offline, on a single machine, with zero accounts.

### Open by default

MIT licensed. Fork it. Modify it. Ship it in your product. Build something better. The whole point is that this technology should be accessible, not locked behind a subscription.

## What we won't build

- **Deception features** — no voice cloning that mimics a specific person without their knowledge
- **Surveillance tools** — no hidden recording, no transcription sent to third parties
- **Attention theater** — no fake "I'm here" presence that pretends you're actively listening when you're not connected at all

## What we will build

- Better voices, more languages
- Direct Google Meet / Zoom join (no BlackHole workaround)
- Lip-synced avatar video from a photo
- Memory across meetings
- Phone-based remote control
- Interruption handling (barge-in)

The goal is a meeting assistant that's genuinely useful — not a gimmick, not a toy, not a way to fake being present. A tool that respects both you and the people you're meeting with.
